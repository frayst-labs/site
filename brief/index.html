How each finding is proven

A wrong answer passes

The environment's own grader gives full marks to an answer that breaks a requirement quoted word for word from the task.

It passes every time

In fresh, sealed runs, the wrong answer passes 3 of 3 times and the correct answer still passes 5 of 5.

The fix holds

We change the grader so the wrong answer fails and the correct one still passes, then run both again.
